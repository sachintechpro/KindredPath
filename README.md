# KindredPath

**A personal family notebook for understanding the choices behind family life.**

I started KindredPath because I kept coming back to the same kind of question: *What does this actually mean for a normal family?*

A new baby. A home. A job change. A child heading toward college. A parent who may need care. Medicare getting closer. Retirement. The documents nobody wants to think about until they are suddenly needed.

I wanted a place where I could slow those conversations down.

KindredPath is my attempt to share what I have learned along the way in plain language. I introduce product categories and planning concepts, show possible benefits and trade-offs, let families experiment with simple numbers, and point back to government and institutional sources for the facts that matter.

## What KindredPath is

KindredPath is an **information and exploration site for residential families**.

It helps a person move through this sequence:

**Life question → concept → product/category → benefits → trade-offs → questions to ask → primary source**

It is intentionally not a product catalog, sales site, comparison marketplace, or financial recommendation engine.

## What I cover

- Family protection and life insurance concepts
- Term life, whole life, universal life and IUL
- Disability, critical illness, accident and long-term-care concepts
- 401(k), traditional IRA, Roth IRA and other retirement concepts
- Social Security and family-benefit questions
- 529 and education-savings concepts
- Medicare, Medigap, Medicare Advantage and prescription coverage concepts
- Home ownership and household cash flow
- Emergency savings and debt repayment
- Childcare and parental leave
- Aging parents and caregiving
- Family documents, beneficiaries and legacy planning

## My approach to product education

I do not want to say only what a product *can* do.

For each category I try to show:

- **What it is**
- **Why a family might consider it**
- **Potential benefits**
- **Trade-offs and limitations**
- **Questions I would ask**
- **Where I would verify the information**

That is especially important for products whose illustrations, guarantees, charges, eligibility rules or tax treatment can be misunderstood.

## Sources

I prefer primary public sources for factual and current information.

KindredPath links to resources from:

- U.S. Census Bureau
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
- Medicare.gov / CMS
- Social Security Administration
- Internal Revenue Service
- U.S. Department of Labor
- U.S. Department of Education
- Federal Student Aid

I intentionally do not use insurance carriers, banks, brokerages, product vendors or marketing websites as the authority for the educational claims on the site.

The live **Sources** page is part of the product: it lets a visitor leave my explanation and read the underlying public information.

## The voice

KindredPath is written in first person because it is personal.

I am not presenting myself as the authority on every subject. I am documenting what I have learned, what I still need to verify, the questions I would ask, and the sources I trust enough to send a family to.

Rules change. Costs change. Families change.

The site should change with them.

## Privacy

- No visitor account is required.
- The Future Lab calculations run in the browser.
- The readiness checklist is local to the page and is not submitted.
- The site does not ask for Social Security numbers, account numbers, medical records or other sensitive information.
- Routine hosting logs may exist outside the application.

## Contact

The conversation form is intended for questions about the educational material and the KindredPath project. It is not an application for financial, tax, legal, insurance or investment advice.

## Repository

This repository contains the Flask application and the content/design system for KindredPath.

The important thing about this project is not how it is deployed. It is the **intent**: make complicated family decisions easier to understand, more honest about trade-offs, and easier to verify through public sources.
